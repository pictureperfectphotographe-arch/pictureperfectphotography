## 2. Approved visual direction

- **Look:** Contemporary editorial photography / creative studio, not generic wedding luxury.
- **Base:** White backgrounds and black typography with restrained grey tones and subtle gradients.
- **Photography:** Full-colour originals; imagery carries the emotion and visual colour.
- **Composition:** Floating hero collage, oversized condensed typography, italic serif accents, intentional negative space, asymmetrical image grids.
- **Components:** Minimal navigation, fine dividers, square-corner image panels, service accordion, quiet CTA buttons.
- **Avoid:** Gold, beige, burgundy, heavy shadows, generic rounded cards, loud decorative icons, excessive motion.

### Typesetting rules

- Display: `line-height: 0.9–1.05`, `letter-spacing: -0.02em`; uppercase where appropriate.
- Body: `line-height: 1.5–1.7`; use readable line lengths.
- Uppercase eyebrow labels: `letter-spacing: 0.12–0.18em`.
- Use italic accents selectively within headlines; never set whole paragraphs in display fonts.
- Maintain accessible contrast and responsive wrapping; avoid clipped text on narrow screens.

## 8. Motion and interaction

**Approved motion character:** Subtle editorial, not loud or distracting.

| Interaction | Duration | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Page entrance | 500–700 ms | Fade plus 12–20 px upward translation |
| Image reveal | 600–900 ms | Gentle clip/mask reveal |
| Image hover | 350–500 ms | Scale 1.00 → 1.025 |
| Button hover | 200–250 ms | Minimal colour/background transition |
| Accordion | 250–350 ms | Smooth height expansion |
| Navigation | 200–300 ms | Underline/opacity transition |
| Hero collage | Scroll-linked, very subtle | Maximum 8–16 px displacement |

Recommended easing: `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; avoid autoplay with sound and effects that delay navigation.

## 9. Photography and content rules

- Use the studio's actual photographs and films for the live website; concept imagery is not portfolio evidence.
- Preserve subjects, identity, natural skin tones, colours and original image edits unless explicitly approved otherwise.
- Mix intimate portraits, wider scenes, candid interactions and detail shots.
- Avoid repeating the same photograph excessively.
- Keep subjects visible when cropping (`object-fit: cover` with deliberate focal positions).
- Serve responsive WebP/AVIF when appropriate and lazy-load below-the-fold assets.
- Only publish project names, client photographs and testimonials with appropriate permission.
- Never invent customer counts, project totals, team sizes, ratings or testimonials. **Established in 2021** and **India-wide coverage** are confirmed.

## 10. Accessibility, SEO and measurement

- Semantic heading structure, descriptive alt text, keyboard navigation and visible focus states.
- Responsive and accessible menus, accordions, filters, video controls and forms.
- Aim for Core Web Vitals: LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP ≤ 200 ms, CLS ≤ 0.1 at the 75th percentile.
- Unique SEO pages for actual services; Hyderabad/Madhapur business information and accurate structured data.
- Track service-page visits, portfolio interactions, WhatsApp clicks, form submissions, qualified enquiries and confirmed bookings separately.
- Protect enquiry data, prevent spam, and show a clear form confirmation.
